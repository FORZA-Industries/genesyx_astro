## What is vaginal pH?

pH is a scale from 0 to 14 that measures acidity. Below 7 is acidic, above 7 is alkaline. A healthy vagina
is naturally slightly acidic, typically between **3.8 and 4.5**, which helps protect against infection.

## Why pH matters in the Shettles theory

The Shettles theory proposes that a more acidic environment may favour X-bearing sperm and a more alkaline
environment may favour Y-bearing sperm. This has not been consistently demonstrated in research.

## How Genesyx uses pH readings

Your programme includes at-home vaginal pH test strips. You log each reading in the companion app, so you
can see your readings alongside your cycle timing and nutrition.

## What we will never recommend

- Douching, which the NHS advises against
- Vinegar, baking soda or other home remedies
- Anything that could irritate or harm vaginal tissue

A reading outside the usual range, or symptoms such as unusual discharge, odour or itching, are a reason
to speak to your GP or a pharmacist.
