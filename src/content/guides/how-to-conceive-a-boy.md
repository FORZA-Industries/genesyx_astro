## The honest starting point

Slightly more boys than girls are born worldwide, roughly 105 boys for every 100 girls. The sex of your
baby is decided by whether the sperm that fertilises the egg carries an X or a Y chromosome, and no
natural method has been shown to reliably change that.

## Methods couples commonly try

### 1. Shettles timing

The Shettles method suggests having intercourse **as close to ovulation as possible**, ideally on the day
of ovulation. The theory is that Y-bearing sperm swim faster but survive for less time. Read the full
[Shettles method guide](/shettles-method).

### 2. Vaginal environment

The theory also proposes that a more alkaline vaginal environment may favour Y-bearing sperm. Genesyx
uses at-home [vaginal pH tests](/vaginal-ph) so you have readings, not guesses. **Never douche or use
home remedies** to change your pH.

### 3. Diet

Some small studies have linked diet around conception with a baby's sex, but the findings have not been
reliably replicated. The Genesyx XY nutrition guide focuses on a balanced preconception diet.

## Things to keep in mind

- If you have been trying for over a year (or six months if you are over 35), speak to your GP.
- In the UK, choosing a baby's sex through IVF for non-medical reasons is not permitted.
