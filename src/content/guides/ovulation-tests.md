## What is the fertile window?

Sperm can survive in the body for up to five days, while an egg survives for around 12 to 24 hours after
ovulation. That makes your fertile window roughly **six days**: the five days before ovulation plus the day
you ovulate.

## How ovulation tests work

Ovulation tests measure **luteinising hormone (LH)** in your urine. LH rises sharply, known as the LH
surge, usually **24 to 36 hours before ovulation**. A positive test tells you ovulation is likely soon.

## When to start testing

Ovulation usually happens around 14 days **before** your next period, not 14 days after your last one.
For a 28-day cycle that is around day 14; for a 32-day cycle, around day 18. Start testing a few days
before you expect to ovulate and test at the same time each day.

| Cycle length | Likely ovulation day | Start testing around |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 26 days | Day 12 | Day 9 |
| 28 days | Day 14 | Day 11 |
| 30 days | Day 16 | Day 13 |
| 32 days | Day 18 | Day 15 |

## Other signs of ovulation

- **Cervical mucus** becomes clearer, wetter and stretchier, like raw egg white.
- **Basal body temperature** rises slightly *after* ovulation, so it confirms ovulation rather than
  predicting it.

## When to get advice

Speak to your GP if your cycles are very irregular, your tests never show a surge, or you have been trying
to conceive for over a year (six months if you are over 35).
