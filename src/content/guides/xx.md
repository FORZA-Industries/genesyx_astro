## What's in the XX programme

- **XX Formula:** three monthly packs. [PLACEHOLDER: ingredients and authorised claims.]
- **XX nutrition guide:** a route-specific eating plan for the 90 days.
- **Measure:** vaginal pH test strips, ovulation tests and pregnancy tests.
- **Companion app:** your pH readings, cycle day and timing window in one calm view.

## Timing on the XX route

Following the Shettles method, the XX route focuses on intercourse two to four days before ovulation. The
companion app uses your ovulation test results to show your window.

## Before you start

Genesyx is not a fertility treatment and cannot guarantee a girl. If you have been trying to conceive for
more than a year, or have a health condition, speak to your GP first.
