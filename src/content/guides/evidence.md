## What we claim, and what we don't

**We do:** coordinate the parts of the Shettles approach (nutrition, a supplement formula, at-home
testing and timing) into one clear programme.

**We don't:** claim that any product, food or timing can guarantee, or reliably change, the sex of a baby.

## Key research

- **Wilcox, Weinberg & Baird (1995).** *Timing of sexual intercourse in relation to ovulation: effects on
  the probability of conception, survival of the pregnancy, and sex of the baby.* New England Journal of
  Medicine, 333(23), 1517–1521. Found no association between the timing of intercourse and the sex of the
  baby.
