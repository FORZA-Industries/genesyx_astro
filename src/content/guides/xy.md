## Who the XY programme is for

Couples who are hoping for a boy, want to try the Shettles approach, and would rather follow it with a
clear plan and safe products than with guesswork. It is a wellness programme, not a fertility treatment.

## What's in the XY programme

| Part | What you get | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| XY Formula | Three monthly packs | A route-specific supplement taken through the 90 days |
| XY nutrition guide | A route-specific eating plan | What to eat through the programme |
| Vaginal pH tests | At-home test strips | Readings instead of guesses |
| Ovulation tests | At-home LH tests | Finding your fertile window each cycle |
| Pregnancy tests | At-home tests | Knowing when to stop |
| Companion app | pH, cycle and timing in one view | Keeping every part in step |

## How XY timing works

The Shettles method's XY route is built on timing: intercourse as close to ovulation as possible, often the day of a positive ovulation test and the day after. In the theory, Y-bearing sperm are said to swim faster, so they reach the egg first when ovulation has just happened.
The theory also favours a more alkaline vaginal environment. Genesyx never recommends douching or home remedies
to change pH.

A typical cycle on the XY route looks like this:

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Day 1 of the cycle | Log your period start in the app |
| A few days before expected ovulation | Start daily ovulation tests |
| Until your ovulation test turns positive | The Shettles method suggests avoiding unprotected sex |
| Positive test day and the day after | The XY timing window in the Shettles method |
| About 14 days after ovulation | Take a pregnancy test if your period hasn't arrived |

Your own days depend on your cycle length. Our guide to [ovulation tests](/ovulation-tests) explains how to
find your window, and the companion app works it out from the tests you log.

## What to expect over 90 days

- **Month 1:** start the formula and nutrition guide, learn your cycle and take your first pH and ovulation
  readings.
- **Month 2:** you know roughly when you ovulate, so timing gets easier and the app's window is based on
  your own results.
- **Month 3:** keep going with the same routine; if you have not conceived, the app keeps tracking into the
  next cycle.

The XY route uses the most fertile days, so it does not usually slow conception down. Waiting for a positive test does mean earlier days in the fertile window go unused.

## What the evidence says

Large studies have not found that the timing of intercourse changes a baby's sex, and modern research finds
no meaningful difference between X- and Y-bearing sperm in speed, size or pH response. Genesyx does not
claim to change the odds. It helps you follow the approach consistently and safely. Read the full
[evidence](/evidence) and our guide to [conceiving a girl or boy](/how-to-conceive-a-girl-or-boy).

Hoping for a girl instead? See the [XX programme](/xx).

## Before you start

- Genesyx is not a fertility treatment and cannot guarantee a boy.
- Take folic acid while trying to conceive: the NHS recommends 400 micrograms a day.
- If you take any medicine or have a health condition, check with your GP or pharmacist before starting a
  new supplement.
- See your GP if you have been trying for a year without success, or sooner if you are 36 or over.
