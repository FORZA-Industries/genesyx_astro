## What's in the XY programme

- **XY Formula:** three monthly packs.
- **XY nutrition guide:** a route-specific eating plan for the 90 days.
- **Measure:** vaginal pH test strips, ovulation tests and pregnancy tests.
- **Companion app:** your pH readings, cycle day and timing window in one calm view.

## Timing on the XY route

Following the Shettles method, the XY route focuses on intercourse as close to ovulation as possible. The
companion app uses your ovulation test results to show your window.

## Before you start

Genesyx is not a fertility treatment and cannot guarantee a boy. If you have been trying to conceive for
more than a year, or have a health condition, speak to your GP first.
